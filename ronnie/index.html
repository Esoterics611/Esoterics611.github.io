Venture · research

BlackRock : NYSE :: NewCo : INX

A tokenized FX and fixed-income issuer, worked out from first principles. Design the indices, build defined-risk products on them, issue them as tokens, and rent every regulated function from a venue that already holds the licence.

The gap

The two largest markets in finance have barely been tokenized at all.

$9.6tn

$7.9tn
All tokenized RWAs
~$20bn

The tokenized line is total market value, not daily turnover: at this scale it is a hairline. FX and rates make up almost none of it. Figures from my research, September 2026.

The first product: a shekel that pays

USDC : USDY :: BILS : this

Israel's regulated shekel stablecoin may not pay yield. At a 4.00% policy rate, holding it costs 4% a year.

A shekel-denominated bill fund, tokenized on Base and subscribed and redeemed in USDC. The fund holds US Treasury bills and buys shekels forward. Covered interest parity delivers the shekel rate and the dollar rate cancels, so the token's NAV accrues at roughly the Bank of Israel rate, less a fee. The issuer engine is public.

The principles it runs on

  1. The arbitrage is the product. Price tracks NAV because closing the gap pays, not because anyone promised it.
  2. The floor is ours. Market makers hold rights, not duties, so a capped, issuer-funded redemption facility at NAV minus a published haircut sets the floor and the spread ceiling.
  3. Boring first. Version one is a fully funded bill basket. Each later product adds exactly one regulatory question.
  4. Own the rulebook. ETF sponsors pay index providers; writing our own indices keeps that rent and the fee advantage.
  5. Microstructure has a kill criterion. Coarse ticks or low rate limits each kill the arbitrage; refuse to launch into them.
  6. Honest numbers, or nothing. If the engine cannot compute a product's risk honestly, the product does not ship.

This is independent research and an engineering prototype in my own name. It is not an offer of securities or investment advice, and it does not describe any product of Republic, INX or any other company.
